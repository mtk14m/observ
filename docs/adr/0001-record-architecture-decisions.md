# 0001. Record architecture decisions

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-06

## Context

obsrv makes long-lived choices: storage format, protocols, languages. Contributors need to know
why things are the way they are before they change them.

## Decision

We record every significant architecture decision as an ADR in `docs/adr/`.

## Consequences

Decisions are reviewable in pull requests and discoverable later. Writing an ADR adds a small cost
to big changes, and that cost is intended.
